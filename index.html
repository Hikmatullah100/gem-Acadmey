Helping every learner reach their potential

Gem Academy is presented here for O Level, IGCSE, A Level and selected international exam-board tuition. Confirm your exact subject and exam board with our team.

Can I request a free demo class?

Yes. Submit the enquiry form with your grade and subject. Our team can contact you to discuss availability and the next steps.

Are lessons available online?

This website is designed to promote online tuition. Lesson platform, timings and availability should be confirmed with the academy.

How much do lessons cost?

Fees depend on level, subject, lesson frequency and tutor availability. Contact us for a personalised quote.

Yes, students can enquire from different locations. Please include your country and time zone so scheduling can be discussed.
